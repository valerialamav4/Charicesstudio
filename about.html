Meet Charice Sinclair!

Charice Sinclair is a mixed-media and multimedia independent artist. Her repertoire includes an associate's degree in graphic design from College of Southern Nevada, a Bachelor's in psychology from Georgia State University, and specialties in sculpture and textiles.

She sells her handmade art on e-commerce platforms like Etsy and Depop. She also sells wares once a month at the renowned First Friday Festival in the Las Vegas Arts District.

History of Charice’s Studio

Charice’s Studio began on Etsy in 2014 as Crafting4Wings, inspired by the band Tool’s song “Wings for Marie”. Initially the business started as a side hustle, selling hand-made figurines, Oreo necklaces, and beaded jewelry. Pausing briefly while Charice attended university, and though it briefly reopened in 2016 as Crafting4Dopamine, she stepped back again that same year.

From 2016 to 2024, she created most of her art privately as her hobby. In 2022 she launched a new brand, Ethnic & Eclectic, vending vintage clothing in Denver and earning an average of $500 on Depop’s e-commerce site, though this business name did not last much longer.

After moving to Las Vegas in 2023 and returning to school for Graphic Design, Charice decided to open her business once again, rebranding as Charice’s Studio.

She started vending at craft fairs, hosting her own craft classes, altering clothes, and making her original clay jewelry, successfully growing her business once again. With a fresh degree in Graphic Communications, Charice has expanded her business into social marketing for her brand and associates.

Though small, this one-woman enterprise has amassed a local loyal customer base, and in 2027 Charice has plans to expand the e-commerce, bespoke clothing alterations, and Studio-store space in order to expand the team and business at Charice’s Studio.

Charice keeps her social media presence up-to-date with her latest projects and events, for more information please check out her Contact and Events page.
