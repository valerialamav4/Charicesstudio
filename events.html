First Friday!

On pause through the end of 2026

First Friday Las Vegas is paused through the end of the year while the First Friday Foundation, the City of Las Vegas and local law enforcement work on bringing it back safely. Check the First Friday website for its return, and keep supporting local artists in the Arts District in the meantime.
